Why we verify identity
Accessing another person's health records is a sensitive action regulated by HIPAA and state privacy laws. Identity verification ensures only legitimate guardians can act on behalf of a minor.

  • Required by federal and state law

When a minor caregivee reaches age 18, the guardian-based minor relationship must be removed and replaced with an adult mutual-consent relationship. Both parties are notified.
👩 Vani Nagam — Parent/Caregiver
🎂 Emma Nagam — Turns 18 · Now an adult
⚡ System-triggered event
⚠️
System trigger: Emma Nagam turns 18 on June 14, 2033. The minor caregiving relationship will automatically expire and both parties will be notified 30 days in advance.
📅
T-30 days
Advance Notice
Health100 sends notifications to both Vani and Emma. Explains what will change and what action is needed.
→
🔒
On 18th birthday
Minor Relationship Expires
Guardian-based minor relationship automatically removed. All minor-specific permissions revoked. Access suspended pending new consent.
→
📨
Step 3
Adult Consent Request
Vani may request a new adult caregiving relationship. Emma receives the request and must actively consent as an adult.
→
✋
Step 4
Emma Decides
Emma — now an adult — chooses which services to share with Vani. She has full control. She may grant Pharmacy only, or all services.
→
✅
Step 5
Adult Relationship
New adult relationship established with Emma's consent. Permissions reflect her choices, not guardian defaults.
✅
Outcome
Privacy transition handled automatically. The platform enforces that adults control their own data — no guardian can retain access without the adult's explicit re-consent. Both parties notified at every step.
